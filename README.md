## Content

In this course, students will bring a problem from their PhD or master’s project to share with the class. Students will get tailored advice about the appropriate statistical analysis/approach for their data in this course. They will work on their problem with guidance from the instructor and peer support through regular online meetings and one intermediate meeting. At the end of the course, students will present their solution to the class.

Workshop 1: Introduction and organization, students’ presentation of their problem.
Workshop 2: Intermediate meeting to discuss progress and share strategies.
Workshop 3: Final presentations of students’ projects, discussion and feedback.
Week 2-Week 11: Weekly online tutoring (depending on the number of students, 3-6 sessions per student)

The workshops (2 hours each) will take place at the University of Basel on September 16th 2026, October 28th 2026, and Wednesday December 9thh 2026 from 1 pm to 3 pm.

The individual online weekly sessions will take place on Wednesdays from 1:30 to 3 pm.


## Learning Objectives

-	Students will gain hands-on experience using GitHub within RStudio for version control and collaboration
-	Students will develop an R script tailored to analyze their specific research problem
-	Students will contribute to a shared repository of R scripts, enhancing collective learning and code reusability

## Organisation

**Admission requirements:** Primarily for PhD students at the Department of Environmental Sciences and PSC PhD students (Master’s students may join with permission).

**Prior Knowledge:**
Basic knowledge of R (for example, attendance of the 48662 Introduction to R or the PSC course “Introduction to R”) as well as a basic course in statistics.

Before the start of the course, please send an email to natacha.bodenhausen@unibas.ch including your PhD program or master’s degree and a short description of your problem (5 sentences maximum).


**Number of Participants:** Participation is limited to 8 students (Priority given to DUW and PSC PhD students)


**Assessment:** Active participation includes a mandatory 6 hours of in-person engagement, 4-6 individual online tutoring sessions, and the submission of an R script presenting the solution to the problem.
